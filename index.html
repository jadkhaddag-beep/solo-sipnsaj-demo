Our Story

A little shop in Verdun with a big “from scratch” obsession.

Every morning, before the first coffee is poured, the dough is kneaded fresh. No shortcuts, no preservatives — just flour, water, love and the sound of saj hitting the hot dome.

We took everything we love about Lebanese street food and made it lighter: our saj and baguettes are pressed with 0% oil, our recipes carry 0% added sugar, and everything leaves the grill hot, folded and sealed — ready for your solo trip home.

  • Daily fresh dough — «ريحة الصاج بلّشت من الصبح»
  • Kneaded without oil, sweetened without sugar
